## Evidence (complete Stage 6)

1. Hybrid real geography/traffic/demographics + synthetic pickups documented.
2. Fair comparison: **816** runs on **68** scenarios; SOC35 paired RACE − greedy Δ ≈ **−0.010**, 95% CI **[−0.019, −0.002]**.
3. RACE search selects ≈ **0** optimized plans; high timeouts / runtime.
4. Matched traces show greedy-only successes under identical request streams.
5. Sensitivities (spatial, hourly, candidates, fleet, SOC) do not establish RACE superiority.
6. No in-repo observed courier orders; locked_final held out untuned.

## Recommendation

**Primary: B.**

- Honest negative methodological result for RACE-RH.
- Value in geographically grounded synthetic online EVRP + charging-policy evidence.
- **Not A.**  
- **C** only as secondary framing (reusable release + eval_v1; not a flagship courier benchmark).  
- **D** only if authors insist on real-demand claims.

## Forbidden claims

Real parcel-order dataset; digital-twin travel times; RACE-RH superiority; that synthetic expansion equals empirical representativeness.
