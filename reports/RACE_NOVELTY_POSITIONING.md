# RACE-COMM — Novelty positioning (draft)

## Not claimed as novel by themselves

Multi-agent bidding; event-triggered messaging; MARL/GNN for EVRP; Casablanca geography alone.

## Candidate distinction (to be stress-tested in Phase 5–7)

**Energy-certified, task-specific EV bids under irreversible commitments**, with a **decision-relevant selective trigger**, evaluated against the **same assignment rule** under none / periodic / full / random communication, including counterfactual claim-change metrics and matched-budget checks.

## Literature anchors to confront

- Hybrid MARL + VNS dynamic EVRP (Computers & OR, 2025).
- Transformer MARL electric logistics (Applied Energy, 2026).
- Communication-aware transportation MARL (TRC, 2025).
- Importance-aware / sparse multi-agent communication.

A defensible TRC claim requires Phase 5 scale + an ablation that isolates the energy-certificate / selective-trigger integration—not architecture diagrams.
