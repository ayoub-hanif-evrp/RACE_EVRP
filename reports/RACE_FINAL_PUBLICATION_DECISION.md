## Evidence summary

1. **Zhao et al. 2026 (AC-DMTA)** already covers interval bids, winner-stability certificates, decision-triggered communication, delay, and loss. ECOM certificates are **not sufficiently novel** as a general principle (`RACE_FINAL_CLOSEST_PRIOR_ART.md`).
2. Delayed OT losses are caused by **deferral to stale peer bids**, not by byte volume; assignment wait is shared across modes (`RACE_FINAL_DELAY_FAILURE_DIAGNOSIS.md`).
3. On **60 development scenarios**:
   - Delay 0: selective = full OT at ~25% of full bytes; ECOM adds bytes without OT gain.
   - Delay 30/120: unmodified selective/ecom/full lose to none; **selective_deadline / ecom_deadline** recover to within **2 pp** of none and beat selective with large byte cuts.
4. PROVED certificate counts remain ~0 — do not sell certificate dominance as the empirical contribution.
